⚡ Paper 5 High-Yield Statutory Forms, e-Forms & Thresholds

Quick reference for Companies Act 2013, LLP Act, Industrial Laws & Ethics

🏛️ Key Companies Act e-Forms (Sec 1–122)

  • INC-3: Nominee consent in OPC | INC-4: Change of nominee (15 days)
  • INC-6: Conversion of Pvt Co ↔ OPC
  • INC-8 / INC-9: Declaration by professionals / subscribers
  • INC-11: Certificate of Incorporation | INC-20A: Commencement of business (180d)
  • INC-12: Sec 8 Licence | INC-13: Sec 8 MOA | INC-18: Sec 8 Conversion
  • INC-22: Registered Office verification (30 days) | INC-23: RD approval shift RO
  • PAS-3: Return of Allotment | PAS-4: Private Placement Offer Letter
  • SH-1: Share Certificate | SH-3: Sweat Equity | SH-4: Share Transfer
  • SH-7: Alteration of Share Capital | SH-8/9/11: Buy-Back Offer, Solvency, Return
  • CHG-1 / CHG-9: Charge Registration (30d) | CHG-4: Satisfaction of Charge
  • MGT-1: Register of Members | MGT-7 / 7A: Annual Return (60d from AGM)
  • MGT-11: Proxy Form (48 hrs before) | MGT-12: Polling Paper | MGT-14: Special Resolution (30d)

👔 Directors, Audit & Industrial Law Numbers

  • Sec 149 Directors: Min 1 (OPC), 2 (Pvt), 3 (Public); Max 15 (SR for >15)
  • Sec 152(6) Rotation: ≥ 2/3rd rotational in Public Co; 1/3rd retire every AGM (Ind. Directors excluded)
  • Sec 166 Duties of Directors: Fine ₹1 Lakh to ₹5 Lakh; Undue gain = refund equivalent amount
  • Sec 168 Resignation: Company files DIR-12 (30d); Director may file DIR-11 (30d)
  • Sec 197 Managerial Remuneration: Overall 11% Net Profit; 1 MD/WTD = 5%; >1 MD/WTD = 10%; Non-Exec = 1% (with MD) or 3% (without MD); Sitting fee Max ₹1 Lakh
  • Sec 177 Audit Committee: Listed + Public Co (Paid-up ≥ ₹10 Cr, Turnover ≥ ₹100 Cr, Loans/Deb/Dep > ₹50 Cr); Min 3 directors, majority Independent
  • Factories Act: With power ≥ 10; Without power ≥ 20; Adolescent: 15–18 yrs; Max 48 hrs/wk, 9 hrs/day; Spread-over 10.5 hrs; Drinking water cool >250; Canteen >250; Creche >30 women; Welfare Officer ≥500; Safety Officer ≥1000
  • Gratuity Act: ≥10 employees; 5 yrs continuous (240d, or 190d underground); Rate = (Basic+DA)×15/26; Max Ceiling ₹20 Lakhs; Paid within 30 days
  • EPF & ESI: EPF ≥20 emp, 12% Basic+DA (10% sick/jute), EPS 8.33%, Wage ceiling ₹15,000; ESI ≥10 emp, Employer 3.25% + Employee 0.75%, Wage ceiling ₹21,000
